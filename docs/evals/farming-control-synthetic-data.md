# Synthetic risk scenarios for the Farming Control agent benchmark

Design for the [FC agent benchmark](./farming-control-agents.md). The first deliverable should be a small, reproducible set of decision problems for a lender, an LP and a vault depositor. Each problem asks what an operator could reasonably conclude at a specific time. A later turn can change that conclusion.

## How an incident becomes a case

1. **Select a gap.** Start from commissioned FC profile families. Cross their economic dependencies with incident mechanisms, evidence availability and operator questions. For example, “collateral transfers restricted at one address” and “redemption queue delayed for every holder” need different cases even though both might be described as frozen collateral.
2. **Research the chronology.** A research agent collects dated protocol or issuer statements, governance records and published measurements. A second researcher checks scope, alternate explanations, comparisons and timing. Store a claim ledger with source URL/version, passage or table locator, event time or interval, earliest verified public availability, affected operation/entity, unit, and confidence. Record disputes and missing timestamps. If only a retrospective account exists, its facts can calibrate a world while its text becomes visible only on its publication date.
3. **Extract a mechanism.** Describe prerequisites, an initiating event, conditional propagation, a plausible recovery branch and a benign lookalike. Label each parameter as measured in the source, derived by an explicit formula, or chosen for the exercise. Preserve ranges and units across venues. Historical analogies require a written mapping to the target protocol and a list of mechanics that differ.
4. **Generate offline.** Sample an initial state and an event schedule from a reviewed, versioned recipe. Apply the same seed, starting state, background flows and observation noise to a paired control. Override one cause and recompute its descendants. Choose named random streams for world events, observation failures and wording so changing the question cannot change balances. Separate ordinary, stressed and recovery regimes. Incident frequencies in the pack are test coverage choices and carry no probability interpretation.
5. **Publish a pack.** Review the chronology, identities, arithmetic, observable evidence differences and scoring criteria. Store public research and synthetic identities only. A later research pass can propose another revision with new evidence. Scored trials pin an immutable revision and run with offline evidence sources. Hold out incident mechanisms or entire historical analogues as well as seeds to measure generalisation.

## First protocol families

| Position and inputs | How the world produces evidence | Operator judgement to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Curve stablecoin and ETH-denominated LPs, including receipt wrappers | Track coin ordering/decimals, balances, minted and burned LP supply, stored rates, both sides of flows and size-specific exit quotes. Price a wrapper via its verified receipt-to-LP conversion at the same anchor. Use recorded or adapter-derived quotes with declared sizes and validity. | Distinguish turnover from sustained reserve drain, a relative-price change from which coin moved, and a small probe from an actionable position-sized exit. For a pool with no verified position link, report pool exposure only. |
| Morpho Blue isolated lending market | Pin market identity, asset roles, LLTV, configured oracle and its units, borrower distribution, accrued debt/supply, market cash and lender claim. Record reference value, oracle output and executable collateral prices separately. Replay eligibility, successful liquidation, repayment and recognized bad debt as distinct events. | Which borrowers are near liquidation? Is collateral sale capacity deteriorating? How much can the tagged lender withdraw now? A lender's own withdrawal route and a borrower's health factor answer different questions. |
| Morpho USDC vault | Pin vault generation, share decimals, fee-aware redemption preview, idle assets, allocations and available withdrawal routes. Propagate a shock through a documented allocation edge. Track route limits, gates, completeness and quote time. | Distinguish NAV impairment, concentration, and immediate cash availability. Compare an affected underlying market with the same shock outside the vault's allocations. Vault version determines how liquidity is verified. [Morpho withdrawal mechanics](https://docs.morpho.org/developers/earn/tutorials/force-deallocate/) |

Represent each dependency with typed endpoints and the operation it permits, such as transfer, redeem, bridge, value collateral or withdraw underlying. Opening a collateralized loan normally encumbers collateral. A transfer restriction on its custodian, a paused redemption route, and a borrower who can repay to release pledged collateral produce different worlds. A cascade crosses an edge only if that operation or exposure transmits the change. An independent price source requires its own update rule.

For new profiles, pin oracle kind and update policy per deployment. A market feed may update after its heartbeat or deviation condition, a time-weighted price depends on its lookback, and a conversion-rate feed can remain unchanged throughout a secondary-market selloff. Bind feed timestamps, units, scaling and fallback behavior to the profile. Unknown configuration supports an explicitly generic exercise. A named-deployment replay requires a verified configuration at the chosen block.

## A worked Morpho decision

All amounts and times below are synthetic. Consider one borrower with 100 collateral tokens, 70 units of debt and LLTV 0.80. Prices are debt-token units per collateral token. The illustrative market uses a market-linked oracle. Assume no interest in this short interval. Rows are pre-settlement snapshots. A delayed price update changes liquidation eligibility without recording a liquidation or lender loss.

| Time | Configured oracle | Executable price for 100 collateral | Oracle health factor | What the evidence supports |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 10:00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.143 | Position outside the liquidation threshold. |
| 10:15 | 0.95 | 0.80 | 1.086 | Exit depth/price has deteriorated. Oracle still places the borrower above threshold. |
| 10:30 | 0.85 | 0.79 | 0.971 | Borrower eligible for liquidation. Compare completed repayments and remaining eligible debt at the next anchor. |
| 10:30 paired control | 0.95 | 0.79 | 1.086 | Same quote and earlier history. The oracle update is delayed. Economic exit stress persists and oracle-based eligibility has yet to cross. |

Compute the displayed factor as collateral × configured price × LLTV / debt, retaining exact inputs. A current quote for the full collateral returns 79 debt units at 10:30. Eligibility coexists with gross sale proceeds above debt in this example. Claiming realized bad debt would require settlement evidence. With an expired quote, the agent can identify the exposed market and the liquidatable borrower while qualifying the amount recoverable.

To extend this into a deleveraging cascade, use multiple borrowers at different distances from LLTV. Settlement repays loan tokens into the market and transfers collateral to a liquidator. If that collateral is sold, update venue depth from the sale. A second borrower crosses only if a subsequent change to its configured oracle or debt warrants it. Record each causal parent and block. Branch on sufficient exit depth versus constrained depth. A lender-facing question additionally needs that lender's claim and available market cash. With synthetic supply of 1,000, debt of 600 and a lender claim of 100, immediate accounting capacity is 100 before any further withdrawal. These figures describe the whole market, including the example borrower.

For the multi-turn case ask “Should we reduce this position?” at the first stressed anchor. Then ask “What changed?” after the eligible-debt, repayment and liquidity observations arrive. Accept a supported hold, trim or exit as appropriate to the evidence and the synthetic operator's stated liquidity need. Require an explanation of the condition that would change the decision. Grade a percentage only when the holding size, available route and sizing constraints support it.

## Ownership and artifact contract

| Seam | Current consumer and proposed use |
| --- | --- |
| FC domain and adapters | FC already owns exact quantities, protocol reads and monitoring DTOs. In FC testkit, generate neutral protocol states and strict responses keyed by operation, arguments and anchor. An unhandled read produces a fixture error. |
| FC evaluation and evidence | Feed generated facts into the existing Curve, Morpho Blue and Morpho vault evaluators. Compose results using the existing hot evidence service and its injected clock/history. The product computes assessments. A direct synthetic HTTP example exercises only the Ragazzo layer and carries that coverage label. |
| Serialization and delivery | Proposed boundary is a versioned offline JSON export from FC testkit. Ragazzo consumes it through loopback fixture HTTP using its real Farming Control adapter, tool permissions, tags, visibility revisions and compactors. This keeps FC's Bun workspace independent of Ragazzo's Node runner. |
| Runtime and data | Isolated Ragazzo trial owner and existing disposable eval database. Synthetic FC state lives in the pack. Production schema, migrations, supervisor, protocol registry, UI and Settings require no changes for the first suite. |
| Trial and grading | Extend the existing Ragazzo eval runner. Capture tool results actually delivered after compaction and pagination. Run future-dated public research through the same visibility clock. The scoring process alone reads hidden truth. |

Each pack declares schema version, generator/FC revision, profile/configuration hash, seed and random stream versions, clock origin, and artifact digests. Export three independently addressable artifacts:

- **Public synthetic inputs** contain identities, units, state transitions and observation transcripts for deterministic regeneration. The fixture transport mounts only the turn-appropriate FC envelopes and public source excerpts. IDs, URLs served to the agent and names omit outcome or variant labels.
- **Private grader inputs** contain the causal ledger, pair mapping, source facts, permissible conclusions by turn, and contradicted claims. Publication is possible for development sets. Held-out cases and grader inputs stay outside the model's tool and filesystem scope.
- **Trial evidence** records the tag/holding/monitor join, accessible versus fetched evidence, verbatim synthetic tool outputs or content-addressed equivalents, final answer and score. This distinguishes missing FC coverage, an adapter dropping a field, and a specialist ignoring an available read.

Keep event/block time, source publication, feed update, collection, and availability-to-agent as separate timestamps. Require a finalized observation when the evaluator requires finality. Respect fresh current evidence and same-lineage historical points. For example, a numeric material trend needs at least three distinct finalized points spanning 30 minutes, qualifying changes and a fresh endpoint. Issue a separate observation-failure twin with identical economics. Dated evidence remains available as history when the corresponding read supports it.

## First acceptance run

1. Start with a lending cascade, stablecoin settlement impairment, ETH LP or wrapper inventory loss, address-scoped restriction, and vault withdrawal pressure. Give each a causal control and at least one checkpoint before the cause is confirmed. Add a healthy but unreachable monitor as a cross-family control.
2. Regenerate twice and compare canonical bytes. Independently verify token-unit conservation including external sinks/sources, mint/burn and explicit losses, share/asset rounding, nonnegative balances, block/time order, feed units, position identity and every claimed exposure edge. An oracle change alone must leave cash unchanged.
3. Check the emitted FC envelope and actual Ragazzo tool result at both checkpoints. For each case record whether the decisive fact is exposed, accessible through a follow-up tool, or unavailable. Recognize a missing capability as a product finding. Check that a wrong-wallet join, future publication leak and amount/decimal mutation each fail their intended checks.
4. Compare economic and observation twins. The correct action can remain identical while the required explanation changes. Reject packs with indistinguishable delivered evidence when the rubric requires different conclusions. Include a fluent unsupported exit and a needless abstention in judge calibration. Report counts by mechanism, protocol and coverage level.
5. Review the first baseline with an operator before promoting new variants. A failed case should return the exact question, evidence delivered, first wrong inference, and a defensible answer at that checkpoint. Retain a held-out slice for future changes to specialist instructions or deterministic risk policy.

The existing FC Curve regression arrays and msUSD fixture supply narrow starting examples for peg recovery, mark deterioration and gaps. The msUSD fixture's flow summary is not a complete event ledger and its existing consumer exercises the mark path. A historical replay claim needs source captures, block ranges and exercised output paths. Until then, publish these variants as synthetic analogues.
